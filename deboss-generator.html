Volunteer steps
  1. Close the notebook, front cover up, on the press bed (white die pad under it).
  2. Lay the template on the cover: slide it until the lips touch the fore-edge, and the top and bottom lips hug the cover. The arrow points to the top of the notebook.
  3. Turn the stamp logo-side down and drop it in the window, with the key tab in the notch.
  4. Centre it under the upper plate and pull the handle down once, firmly, to the stop. Don't press twice.
  5. Lift the stamp straight up, then the template. Check the impression.
Print settings that worked (Bambu H2S, 0.2 nozzle)
  • Stamp: print as exported (face up). 0.08–0.12 mm layers, 4+ walls, 40%+ infill so the relief can't crush. Ironing on the top surface optional.
  • Template: 0.2 nozzle or 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, default walls. Prints flat with the lips pointing up — no supports.
  • Keep back thickness and relief height the same for every stamp, so the press stop never needs re-setting.
